Her story

Ezike Kenechi Judith, popularly known as judith kaccy and affectionately called Mama Ejima, is a Tiktok content creator and a passionate and skilled hairdresser known for her engaging family-oriented content online.

She is recognised for her entertaining family content, hairstyling skills, and the authentic personality she brings to social media.

As a Chancellor, she also offers advice and guidance to people, sharing her experience and words of encouragement when needed.

Through her creativity, dedication, and connection with her audience, Judith Kaccy continues to build a distinctive presence both online and within her community.
